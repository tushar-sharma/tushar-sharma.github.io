Despelote
45 min Started playing this game due to FIFA World cup 2026. It's a love letter to the love of football. It follows the journey of Ecuador football team. The gameplay is minimal but storytelling is amazing.
Stray
9.5 hrs You play as a stray cat in a cyberpunk city. I started playing this game because of my cat, hiccup. The game is beautiful and the puzzles are fun enough not to be frustrating. The ending is nice and heartwarming.
Alpaca Stacka
- It's a cute short little fetch quest. Gameplay is simple where you play as an alpaca, Paz, tasked with collecting five lost chicks for their mother, Mama Hen
Beyond Two Souls
- Made by Quantic Dream. You play as a little girl and her spirit. Choices affect the story.
Hades
- It's a good rogue-like game. The combat rooms are procedurally generated. You get more powerful after you die. You can enable god mode which just increases your damage resistance by 2 percent every time you die till 80%.
Alan Wake2
- You need a GPU with mesh-shader support to run the game. The atmosphere is genuinely terrifying, and the cinematography in the cutscenes is top-tier. The Mind Place is a clever system for organizing clues and objectives. Combat can feel a bit clunky, though enabling One-Shot Mode helps smooth the experience. Exploration in dark areas occasionally drags, but the overall presentation is stunning. Abandoned.
Rayman Legends
26.3 hrs One of the best platformers ever made. Fantastic background music and smooth, fun gameplay. Local co-op. Ubisoft launcher requires you to be online to start the game. The `Challenge mode` is no longer available on Steam due to Ubisoft server shutdown details.
Sunset Overdrive
25.2 Made by Insomniac Games. Cons: Corrupt file message pops up everytime you start the game. The game is goofy and fun. You can even shoot a dog at enemies.
Tomb Raider 2013
- Developed by Crystal Dynamics. This is the first game in the rebooted Tomb Raider series. The gameplay and story is amazing.
The Were Cleaner
- Free to play. It's a cute game where you play as janitor who is also a warewolf who needs to pay his rent.
My Friend Pedro
- It's a total blast – it's like doing backflips while dual-wielding pistols, all with a talking banana as your guide. It's like Max Payne style slow motion shooting game.
Limbo
- It's a dark, puzzle game in a dystopian world. You play as a small child navigating eerie environments filled with deadly traps.
Inside
- It's both cute and dystopian, with clever puzzles. The unsettling moments will stick with you long after you finish. The graphics are gorgeous, and the gameplay feels smooth and immersive. If you love puzzles wrapped in a dark, haunting atmosphere, this game is definitely for you!
Dispatch
- An interactive story gameplay. Feels like 911 call operator but for superheroes. You are suppose to discharge superheroes based on their abilities based on specific task. Voice action has great production value. There's also mini hacking puzzle games in between
Spiritfarer: Farewell Edition
- Cozy game.

- Local co-op.
River City Girls 2
- Local co-op. Abandoned.
GTA 4
27.6 hrs Open-world action game set in Liberty City, a parody of NYC. Graphics feel dated now, but the story is gripping—following Niko Bellic, an immigrant trying to chase the American dream through crime and missions.
The Curse of Monkey Island
- Classic point-and-click adventure with clever puzzles and great humor. I grew up learning English from its witty dialogues and memorable voice acting.
Windjammers 2
- Score goal using flying disc. Local co-op.
Firewatch
- Walking sim. Most of the time you are talking to your supervisor on the radio. Ambience is peaceful. If you like going on a hike, you probably gonna like this.
It takes two
- Local co-op with split-screen. Graphics are nice and the story is good. You play as a couple who are going through divorce and they are turned into dolls. You need to work together to solve puzzles.
Life is Strange Episode 1
- It's a interactive story game. I like the concept of reversing time.
Max Payne II
- Favourite shooting game in the series. You play story of both Max and Mona. Writing and game play is top notch.
Papers, Please
- You play as an immigration officer who has to check passport of visitors into a fictional country of Arstotzka. The game is an example of empathy game as your choices affect the lives of people.
The Walking Dead Season 1
- Gameplay was okish and it's too short
Undertale
- Great Indie game. Graphics are minimal but story and humor is great.
Wreckfest
- Fun wrecking racing game. You can race like a sofa or a lawnmower.
The Witcher 3
45 hrs Great RPG where you play as a witcher haunted by dreams from his past. The story follows Geralt as he searches for his lost adopted daughter, Ciri, while also trying to reunite with the love of his life, Yennefer. The gameplay is a mix of horseback riding, sword combat, and powerful magic.
Sayonara Wild Hearts
- It's a rhythm game with great soundtrack. However, gameplay was unique but too short for the pricel.
NFS Heat
- It fells like arcade racing. Drifting is the most fun part of the game. It has both day and night mode. However, game requires you to be online everytime you play it if you purchased it form Steam.
Hotline Miami
- Fun top down shooter game. The soundtrack is amazing. I binged played it.
Heavy Rain
- Made by Quantic Dream, this is an interactive detective story where you play as a father trying to solve the mystery of his son's disappearance. Your choices matter, and many important moments rely on quick-time events (QTEs), which can be quite challenging. Some controls require you to shake the controller, which felt awkward and became tiring after a while. Despite that, the story kept me engaged from beginning to end.
Fable - The Lost Chapters
55.7 Best RPG game I played growing up. Good or bad choices affect the story. You can even marry and buy a house while still slaying monsters on the side.
How Fish Is Made
- Free to play. Short, eerie. You just have to propel the fish forward in terms of gameplay.
Alice: Madness Returns
- Gothic inspired visuals are great, but puzzles feel repetitive. Tips: Press `LT + A` to open the umbrella. Press `LB` to shrink and reveal hidden hints. Abandoned.
A Short Hike
- Indie game. It's cute short game where you play as a bird who needs to reach the top of the mountain to get reception for his phone. You improve your hike by collecting golden feathers. Tips: Change amount of pixalation in Options > Graphics > Pixel Size. Press A to glide. Played on Steam Deck.
Max Payne III
- Set in São Paulo, Brazil. Lot of cutscenes. Feels like Rambo on a shooting spree. Controls: `X` to take cover, `LT` to aim, `RT` to shoot, `LS` + `RB` to dive forward, `RS` to trigger bullet time, and `LS` (press) to crouch. Played on Steam Deck.
Atuel
- Free to play. Beautiful short game that's an ode to Atuel river.
Titanfall 2
- One of the best FPS games out there. The combat feels incredible—whether you’re on foot or piloting a Titan, every battle is pure adrenaline. The story builds up beautifully, and the ending is nothing short of spectacular. Cons: The EA launcher can be a bit annoying—it insists on connecting to the internet every time you launch the game on steam deck, and occasionally you’ll get a blank screen. A quick restart usually fixes it, though.
The Expendabros
- Fun free demo of Broforce with a great retro feel and awesome gameplay.
Promise Mascot Agency
- It feels like a Japanese cinematic experience while you build a mascot agency. The Japanese voice acting is excellent, and the English subtitles are great. The writing is hilarious. Cons: Don't expect GTA like driving experience.

These make the teleporter easier to spot during chaotic fights and heavy visual effects.
