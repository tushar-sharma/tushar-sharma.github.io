Dear Vishi, here are my logs for today.

> "When a man cannot find meaning, he numbs himself with pleasure." — Viktor Frankl

Pumpkin used to love blueberries. Nearing the end of his life, however, he stopped eating them. He would just stand and stare at a blueberry. Once, he would have gone crazy at the sight of one—but not anymore. He no longer craved pleasure; he wished to avoid pain. He could eat all the blueberries in the world, yet he would still be in an ailing body.

> "The aim of the wise is not to secure pleasure, but to avoid pain." — Aristotle
