## Hiccups 

My four-month-old British Shorthair, Hiccups, has insurance through Trupanion. His plan cost around $75 a month, with 90% coverage and a $250 deductible. Before trying to lower the price, I needed to understand what those numbers meant.

The deductible isn’t a maximum out-of-pocket limit. With this policy, it’s the amount I must meet for each separate covered condition before reimbursement begins. It doesn’t reset every year for the same condition.

The coverage percentage determines how much of the eligible treatment cost the insurer pays. At 90%, I’m responsible for the remaining 10%, along with the deductible and excluded expenses. Routine care and examination fees aren’t covered under my policy.

Customer support offered two ways to reduce my premium: increase the deductible or decrease the coverage percentage. Initially, I thought lowering the percentage meant fewer conditions would be covered. It actually means I would pay a larger share of covered treatment costs.

I decided that keeping 90% coverage and increasing the deductible to $750 made sense for my budget. The quoted premium was $44.35 a month, saving approximately $368 a year, or $1,839 over five years if prices stayed unchanged. I could put those savings into a vet fund.

There’s a tradeoff: separate conditions can each trigger a deductible, and an illness could happen before I’ve built up those savings. Trupanion also confirmed that once I increase the deductible, I cannot lower it later.

## Edit two lines in NeoVim

Lets say you have two lines 

```

image2: https://unsplash.com/photos/MlrBBF8Er8s/download
```

I want to replace both lines simulateneously. 

1. Put the cursor on the first line.

2. Press Vj to select both lines.

3. :s/MlrBBF8Er8s/2laIaUHt50k/g
