## Retries, backoff, and jitter

A **transient error** is temporary, such as a network timeout or an overloaded service. A retry may succeed. A **permanent error**, such as invalid credentials or invalid input, requires a change before another attempt can succeed.

Retries are safe only when an operation is idempotent. A repeated `GET` should have no additional effect, while retrying `POST /payments` may create a duplicate payment. Use an **idempotency key** when a write may need to be retried.

Use **exponential backoff** between attempts: for example, wait 1, 2, 4, and 8 seconds. Limit both the delay and the number of attempts. Add **jitter**—a small random delay—so clients do not retry simultaneously and create a thundering herd.

## Backpressure and throttling

- **Backpressure:** a slower consumer signals the producer to reduce or pause production. This prevents the consumer from being overwhelmed.
- **Throttling:** a service limits incoming requests to protect its capacity. An HTTP service commonly responds with `429 Too Many Requests` and may include a `Retry-After` header.

Backpressure coordinates the rate between producer and consumer. Throttling enforces a rate limit at the receiving service.

## Solana faucet

A **faucet** distributes free tokens for development and testing; it can be off chain service or smart contract. The [Solana faucet](https://faucet.solana.com/) sends Devnet SOL to a wallet address.
