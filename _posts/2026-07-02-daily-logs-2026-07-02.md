## Spanish Notes

### Feeling

The sentence:

> ¿Cómo te sientes?

means:

> How are you feeling?

Here, **sientes** comes from the verb **sentir**. A common response is:

> Me siento bien.

which means:

> I feel good.

Some forms of this verb are:

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| siento | I feel |
| sientes | you feel |
| sentimos | we feel |
| sienten | they feel |

### Pronouns

We can use pronouns instead of nouns. For example: he, she, and we.

Some basic Spanish pronouns are:

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| yo | I |
| tú | you |
| él | he |
| ella | she |
| nosotros | we |
| ellos | they |

### Reflexive Pronouns

A reflexive pronoun is used when the person doing the action is also receiving the action.

For example:

> He hurt himself.

In Spanish, some reflexive pronouns are:

| Person | Reflexive pronoun |
| --- | --- |
| yo | me |
| tú | te |
| él / ella | se |
| ellos | se |

In the sentence:

> ¿Cómo te sientes?

**te** is the reflexive pronoun.
