## Spanish

we will look a sentence

> como te sientes?

Here it means : How are you feeling?

**sientes** is past tense. We can say like


I feel good = Me siento bien.

siento, sientes, sentimos, sienten.

Pronoun is what we can use instead of a noun. for example, he, she.are

In espaniol, yo, tu, el, ellas, nosotros, ellos.

### Reflexive pronoun

person doing the action is also receiving it like 

> He hurt himself.

in espaniol, yo becomes me 
              tu -> te 
              el / ella -> se 
              ellos -> se
