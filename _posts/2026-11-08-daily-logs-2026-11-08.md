## STI Test

In North Carolina, STI testing are public health services provided free of charge. For example Durham County Department of Public Health has [website](https://dconc.gov/Public-Health/Clinical-Services/STD-HIV-Testing/STD-STI-Testing-and-Treatment?utm_source=chatgpt.com) for scheduling free STI testing.  Charlotte city's [website](https://health.mecknc.gov/clinical-services/STI-testing-treatment?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

Afterwords go to  [myChart](https://www.myuncchart.org/MyChart/Home) to see you result.
