## STI Test

In North Carolina, STI testing is a public health service provided free of charge. For example, the Durham County Department of Public Health has a [website](https://dconc.gov/Public-Health/Clinical-Services/STD-HIV-Testing/STD-STI-Testing-and-Treatment) for scheduling free STI testing, as does the [City of Charlotte](https://health.mecknc.gov/clinical-services/STI-testing-treatment).

Afterward, go to [My UNC Chart](https://www.myuncchart.org/MyChart/Home) to see your results.
